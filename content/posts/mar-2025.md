### Stars Added ⭐

- Starred [files.vc](https://gofmhy.tech/file-tools#file-hosts) in File Hosts. 10GB per file, no expiration, unlimited files.

- Starred [AB Download Manager](https://gofmhy.tech/file-tools#download-managers) in Download Managers. Manager that is open-source, well-maintained, feature-rich, and nice to look at.

- Starred [lossless-cut](https://gofmhy.tech/video-tools#video-editors) in Video Editors. Feature-rich editor that preserves quality/metadata for FFmpeg.

- Starred [wide.video](https://gofmhy.tech/video-tools#online-editors) in Online Video Editors. Feature-rich and stands out a lot compared to similar options.

- Starred [Footballia](https://gofmhy.tech/video#sports-replays) in Sports Replays. Full classic football matches.

- Starred [Geotastic](https://gofmhy.tech/gaming#geoguessr-games) in GeoGuessr Games. Seems to stand out compared to similar options.

- Starred [DAREBEE + r/BodyweightFitness Wiki](https://gofmhy.tech/misc#workout-exercise) in Workout. Multiple fitness routines; both seem to be well-liked by the community.

- Re-starred [Stacher](https://gofmhy.tech/social-media-tools#youtube-downloaders) in YouTube Downloaders. People felt as though a good yt-dlp GUI was worth having starred again.

- Starred [UVR5_UI](https://gofmhy.tech/ai#voice-removal-separation) in Voice Removal. Online voice removal tool that seems to work well.
